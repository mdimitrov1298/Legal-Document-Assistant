On 15 January 2025, Greenfield Analytics Ltd (Client) and BrightCode Solutions Ltd (Provider) entered into an IT Services Agreement for the design, development and deployment of a bespoke data-analytics dashboard. The Platform must support 500 concurrent users and process 10,000 records per hour. The total fee is GBP 120,000 payable in three instalments: GBP 30,000 on signing, GBP 45,000 on Phase 2 completion, and GBP 45,000 on Phase 3 acceptance. Phase 2 was due by 30 June 2025. A beta was delivered on 18 July 2025 with deficiencies: only 200 concurrent users, 6,500 records/hour throughput, and a crashing reporting module. Greenfield sent a written breach notice on 12 September 2025 citing clause 6.1, giving BrightCode until 26 September 2025 to remedy. GBP 75,000 has been paid to date. The Provider warrants conformance for 12 months; confidentiality survives 5 years; IP vests in the Client on full payment. The agreement is governed by English and Welsh law.
