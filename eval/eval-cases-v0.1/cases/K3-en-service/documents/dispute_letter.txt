RE: IT SERVICES AGREEMENT DATED 15 JANUARY 2025 — NOTICE OF BREACH

I write on behalf of Greenfield Analytics Ltd regarding the above Agreement.

As you are aware, Phase 2 (Development and testing) was due for completion by 30 June 2025. Your team delivered a beta version on 18 July 2025, eighteen days after the contractual deadline. Following our internal review, we identified the following deficiencies:

1. The Platform supports only 200 concurrent users, well below the agreed minimum of 500.
2. The data processing throughput was measured at approximately 6,500 records per hour, falling short of the contractual requirement of 10,000 records per hour.
3. The reporting module crashes when more than three filters are applied simultaneously.

On 25 July 2025 we provided a written deficiency report to your project manager, Mr David Park. Mr Park acknowledged the issues by email on 28 July 2025 and committed to delivering fixes by 15 August 2025. As of today, 12 September 2025, no corrective update has been received.

We have paid GBP 30,000 upon signing and a further GBP 45,000 upon the purported completion of Phase 2, totalling GBP 75,000 to date.

In accordance with clause 6.1 of the Agreement, this letter constitutes formal written notice of material breach. If the deficiencies listed above are not remedied within 14 days of the date of this letter — that is, by 26 September 2025 — Greenfield Analytics Ltd reserves the right to terminate the Agreement and pursue recovery of sums paid for non-conforming work.

We remain willing to resolve this matter amicably and invite you to propose a remediation plan at the earliest opportunity.
