Council Decision (EU) 2016/1841 of 5 October 2016 approves the conclusion of the Paris Agreement on behalf of the European Union. The Paris Agreement was adopted at the 21st COP in Paris (30 November – 12 December 2015) and signed on 22 April 2016. It enters into force after at least 55 UNFCCC Parties accounting for 55% of greenhouse gas emissions deposit instruments of ratification. The Agreement replaces the 1997 Kyoto Protocol and aims to keep global warming well below 2 °C, pursuing efforts towards 1.5 °C. Council Decision (EU) 2015/2071 of 10 November 2015 authorises Member States to ratify the Protocol of 2014 to the Forced Labour Convention, 1930, regarding judicial cooperation in criminal matters. The Protocol supplements the fundamental ILO Convention on forced labour. The EU promotes ratification of ILO conventions to advance human rights, decent work, and the eradication of trafficking in human beings.
