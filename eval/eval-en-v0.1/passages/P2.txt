STATEMENT OF FACTS IN THE ROOF REPAIR DISPUTE

On 3 April 2024 Petar Kolev entered into a contract with "Stroy Invest 2020" EOOD for the repair of the roof of his residential building at 7 Lipa Street, Primerovo. The agreed price was BGN 12,000 including VAT, and the deadline for completion of the works was 31 May 2024. On 5 April 2024 Mr Kolev transferred an advance of BGN 4,000 to the company's bank account.

The works began in mid-April but were interrupted due to rainy weather. The company notified the client of the delay by email on 28 May 2024. The repair was completed on 20 June 2024, and the remaining balance of BGN 8,000 was paid in cash against a receipt on the same day.

On 2 July 2024, after heavy rain, Mr Kolev discovered leaks in the attic near the chimney. On 8 July 2024 he sent a written complaint to the company requesting free-of-charge repair of the leaks. In a letter dated 15 July 2024 the company's manager Nikolay Stoyanov replied that the leaks were caused by cracked plaster on the chimney, which was not part of the contracted repair.
