RULES FOR THE USE OF MUNICIPAL SPORTS HALLS IN THE TOWN OF PRIMEROVO

Art. 1. (1) These rules regulate the procedure and conditions for the use of municipal sports halls by natural and legal persons.
(2) A "user" within the meaning of these rules is a person who has a confirmed reservation for a specific hour.

Art. 2. (1) A reservation shall be submitted in writing or electronically at least three working days before the desired hour.
(2) A single user may reserve no more than four hours per week.

Art. 3. (1) The fee for use is BGN 25 per hour.
(2) For students under 18 years of age the fee is reduced by 50 per cent.
(3) The fee shall be paid no later than on the day of use.

Art. 4. A user who cancels a reservation less than 24 hours before the start of the hour shall owe a penalty of BGN 10.

Art. 5. (1) Entry to the halls is permitted only in sports shoes with clean soles.
(2) Bringing food and glass containers is prohibited.

Art. 6. Compliance with the rules is supervised by the director of the municipal enterprise "Sport Primerovo".
