INSPECTION REPORT ON ROOF EXAMINATION

Today, 22 July 2024, in Primerovo, at 7 Lipa Street, Eng. Elena Dimova, an independent construction expert, carried out an inspection of the roof at the request of the owner Petar Kolev. The inspection was attended by Petar Kolev and Nikolay Stoyanov, manager of "Stroy Invest 2020" EOOD.

The inspection established the following:
1. In the area around the chimney there are three cracked tiles.

3. The plaster on the chimney has surface cracks but shows no signs of water penetration.

According to the expert, the leak in the attic is caused by the unsealed membrane joint and the cracked tiles. The estimated cost of the repair to eliminate the defects is BGN 1,800.
