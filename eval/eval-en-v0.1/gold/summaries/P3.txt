Council Regulation (EC) No 2866/98 of 31 December 1998 establishes the irrevocably fixed conversion rates between the euro and the currencies of the eleven Member States adopting the single currency from 1 January 1999: Belgium, Germany, Spain, France, Ireland, Italy, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Austria, Portugal and Finland. The rates are adopted with six significant figures and expressed as one euro in each national currency, with no inverse or bilateral rates defined. The basis for the rates is the equivalence of one euro to one ECU, as calculated by the Commission on 31 December 1998. The regulation entered into force on 1 January 1999 and is binding in its entirety.
