On 3 April 2024 Petar Kolev contracted "Stroy Invest 2020" EOOD for roof repair at his building in Primerovo for BGN 12,000 including VAT, with a deadline of 31 May 2024. An advance of BGN 4,000 was transferred on 5 April. The works were delayed by rain and completed on 20 June 2024, when the remaining BGN 8,000 was paid in cash. On 2 July 2024 Mr Kolev found leaks near the chimney and sent a complaint, but the company replied that the leaks were caused by cracked chimney plaster not covered by the contract.
