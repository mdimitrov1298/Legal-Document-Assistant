The rules regulate the use of municipal sports halls in Primerovo. Reservations must be submitted at least three working days in advance, and a single user may reserve up to four hours per week. The fee is BGN 25 per hour, reduced by 50% for students under 18, payable on the day of use. Cancellation less than 24 hours before the session incurs a penalty of BGN 10. Entry is allowed only in sports shoes with clean soles, and food and glass containers are prohibited. Compliance is supervised by the director of the municipal enterprise "Sport Primerovo".
