On 22 July 2024 Eng. Elena Dimova inspected the roof at 7 Lipa Street, Primerovo, at the request of owner Petar Kolev. Both Kolev and the company manager Nikolay Stoyanov attended. The expert found three cracked tiles, an unsealed waterproofing membrane joint over 1.5 m, and surface cracks in the chimney plaster with no signs of water penetration. The leak in the attic is attributed to the membrane and the tiles. The estimated repair cost is BGN 1,800. Nikolay Stoyanov refused to sign the report.
