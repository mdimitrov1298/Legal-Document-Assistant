Notes:

- The synthetic passages imitate the style of normative text (P1) and of case
  narratives (P2, P4). They do not reproduce any real act, judgment or
  document. Names, companies, addresses and the town "Примерово" are invented.
- No protected court decisions or documents from real cases are included.
- Claims in `claims/claims.jsonl` are original annotations for this package.
- Publicly available datasets are only used as genre references in the thesis;
  none of their text is included in this draft.
